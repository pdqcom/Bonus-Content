# Update Cadence by Joshua Tree Outcome

How often to update leadership, and when to escalate, based on where you landed on the Joshua Tree.

| Where you landed | Update cadence | Escalate if |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Not affected | Once | New info changes the picture |
| Vendor's problem | When the vendor updates | Vendor goes quiet for 24h on an exploited bug |
| Normal cadence | At completion | Exploitation reports change |
| This week | Daily | It lands on CISA KEV |
| Mitigate | Daily, or on vendor news | Mitigation fails or signs of compromise appear |
| Tonight | Before and after the window | The window goes sideways |
| All hands | Every 1 to 2 hours | Any sign of compromise |
| Incident | Per your IR plan | Already escalated |
